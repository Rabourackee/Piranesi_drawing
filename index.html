How strokes are read

  • │ vertical line becomes a rusticated pier
  • ─ horizontal line becomes a bridge with balustrade
  • ⌒ a gentle hump becomes a curved bridge
  • ∩ an arc becomes an arch and a receding vault
  • ⌐ steps or a diagonal become a staircase
  • ⤴ a sweeping curve becomes a stair that follows it
  • ∪ a sagging curve becomes a chain
  • ≀ a wavy drop becomes a hanging chain and lantern
  • ○ a closed loop becomes a barred window
  • · a dot becomes a tiny figure
  • ~ anything else becomes a rope

The plate

Everything is procedural. Strokes are classified by shape, regularised into architecture, extruded toward the vanishing point, shaded by a fixed light and rendered as layered cross-hatching. The seed makes every plate reproducible.
